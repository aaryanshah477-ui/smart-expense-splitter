Smart Expense Splitter
A web app that splits a restaurant bill fairly between friends. Add friends, list the dishes, tap
who ate what, and see exactly how much each person owes, with GST and service charge
shared in proportion to what they ate.
Live demo:
Features
https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/smart-expense-splitter/
Add or remove friends
Add dishes with price and quantity
Assign each dish to one or more people (shared dishes are split equally)
Adjustable GST and service charge percentages
Per-person breakdown: food share, tax share and total
Warning when a dish is not assigned to anyone
Share the result on WhatsApp or copy it as text
Works on phones and in dark mode
How the split works
1. A dish's cost (price × quantity) is divided equally among the people it is assigned to.
2. Each person's food total is multiplied by (GST % + service charge %) to get their tax
share.
3. Person total = food share + tax share.
